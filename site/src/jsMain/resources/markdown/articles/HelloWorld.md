This is the first post. It exists mostly so that the site has something on it, but it's also a chance to say out loud what this blog is for.

## What you'll find here

Advanced Android engineering, mostly. Kotlin, Compose, dependency injection, architecture at scale, build systems, and the long tail of tooling that makes a large codebase tolerable to work in. Occasional side-trips into Kotlin Multiplatform, web, and whatever else is keeping me up at night that month.

## What you won't find here

Tutorials for beginners, at least not as the primary audience. There are excellent resources for that, and I don't think I have anything new to add. What I do think I have is a decade of specific, hard-won opinions about the tradeoffs at the top of the skill tree - and that is what this blog is for.

## Subscribe, if you like

The RSS feed is the canonical way to follow along. Anything else I might set up (newsletter, social syndication, etc.) will be layered on top of it. Thanks for reading.
