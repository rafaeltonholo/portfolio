A year in Halifax, starting over. I wrote this one for the friends who asked what it actually feels like, rather than the ones who wanted to know about the job.

## The small things

The first month is small things. The grocery store is arranged differently. The coffee is worse and then, later, a little better. The winter air is a different cold - drier, quieter, ruthless to anything exposed, forgiving to anything covered. You learn to layer. You learn to check the weather before deciding whether to walk.

## The bigger things

The bigger things take longer. Work remains work; the meetings run the same regardless of timezone. What shifts is the texture of everything outside work. The friends you had on autopilot aren't next door anymore. Replacing that takes intentional effort, and the effort sometimes feels like a second job.

## What I wouldn't trade

The harbourwalk in every season. The moment the maple leaves turn. The first snowfall of the year, which still feels like a minor miracle. And the feeling of rebuilding a life deliberately, piece by piece, with the people who decided to come along.
